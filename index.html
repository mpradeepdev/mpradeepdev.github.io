About me

Marketing automation, end to end

From audience data to launch day, I build the pieces that make campaigns accurate, compliant, and personal.

I'm a Salesforce Marketing Cloud Developer with 3+ years of experience developing, customizing, and deploying marketing automation solutions. I work across Data Extensions, SQL, Automation Studio, AMPscript, CloudPages, Triggered Sends, and Journey Builder, and I run A/B tests to improve campaign performance.

I also build reusable components, support campaign deployments and production issues, write technical documentation, and develop Proof of Concepts.

Over the past few months I've gone hands-on with Salesforce Data Cloud (Data 360): ingesting data through Data Lake Objects, mapping to Data Model Objects, and applying identity resolution to create a single, accurate customer profile. I'm now expanding into Agentforce and API-based integrations.
